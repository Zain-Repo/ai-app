# AI app logo concepts

## Outcome

Generated two styled raster logo concepts with transparent backgrounds for the
AI app. The second concept is the requested alternate direction: a compact
faceted signal-prism mark with a diamond-shaped negative-space core.

## Affected areas

- `public/branding/ai-app-logo.png`
- `public/branding/ai-app-logo-v2.png`

## Validation

- Confirmed both final files are RGBA PNGs at 1254 × 1254 pixels.
- Confirmed all four image corners are fully transparent.
- Confirmed chroma-key removal produced no detectable near-green pixels in the
  final v2 asset.
- Visually inspected the v2 mark for clean separation, centered composition,
  and readable silhouette.

## Known limitation

These are generated raster concepts rather than editable vector source files.

## Alternate direction — web-informed editorial UI mark

The third concept applies a sharper editorial neo-brutalist interface style:
offset command-tile modules, hard black outlines, flat saturated cobalt and
tangerine fills, and no gradients or glossy effects. Web research used for the
direction emphasized saturated color, bold geometry, and high-contrast
neo-brutalist UI cues.
